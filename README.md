# PROJECT SK — Motorcycle-Powered Shifter Kart

**Independent Student Engineering Project | Mechanical Design | Reverse Engineering | Engine Rebuilding | Fabrication**

![Project SK](images/project-sk-cover.jpg)

## 1. Project Overview

Project SK is an independently developed, motorcycle-powered shifter kart built around a reclaimed **Bajaj Discover 150 DTS-i** motorcycle.

The project explores the transformation of an existing motorcycle powertrain into a functional kart platform through reverse engineering, engine restoration, mechanical design and fabrication.

Rather than purchasing a ready-made kart, the objective is to develop the vehicle by identifying reusable donor components, rebuilding the engine, designing the chassis and fabricating the required mechanical assemblies.

This project is an exercise in practical engineering, resourcefulness and hands-on mechanical development.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Develop a motorcycle-powered shifter kart using a reclaimed donor motorcycle.
- Rebuild and integrate the original motorcycle engine.
- Design and fabricate a tubular chassis.
- Reuse suitable donor components wherever possible.
- Develop the drivetrain and rear mechanical assembly.
- Document the complete engineering and fabrication process.
- Establish a foundation for future structural analysis and performance testing.

## 4. Engineering and Development

### 4.1 Reverse Engineering

The donor motorcycle was dismantled to examine its mechanical assemblies and identify components suitable for reuse.

### 4.2 Engine Rebuilding

The original engine was rebuilt from a seized/non-functional condition and successfully brought back to running condition.

### 4.3 CAD and Mechanical Design

OpenSCAD was used during the chassis design and geometric development process.

The design work focused on adapting the motorcycle powertrain to a kart architecture.

### 4.4 Chassis Fabrication

A tubular chassis was designed, fabricated and welded.

The physical chassis was developed around the packaging and mounting requirements of the powertrain and other mechanical assemblies.

### 4.5 Mechanical Integration

The project involves integration of the engine, drivetrain, rear axle, sprocket arrangement, wheels and supporting mechanical components.
